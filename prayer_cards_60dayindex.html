Prayers For My Wife

Day 1

I am Patient With My Wife

Click to pray

tap to reveal prayer

Father, today I choose patience. I release frustration, quick judgment, and impatience from my heart. I declare that I am slow to anger and quick to listen.

I am patient with my wife's dreams, her pace, her process, and her heart. I respond with grace when she struggles. I lead with understanding, not criticism.

Holy Spirit, fill me with the fruit of patience. Give me eyes to see her perspective, ears to hear her heart, and a voice that builds her up.

I am a patient husband. My patience is a gift of love that makes our home a safe place.

Ephesians 4:2 - "Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love"

Father, I stand as a shield over my wife. I protect her heart, her mind, her body, and her spirit. I cover her with prayer and intentional love.

I am her protector—not controlling, but covering. I guard her from harm, criticism, and anything that diminishes her. I am her safe place.

I am a covering over my marriage. No weapon formed against us will prosper. My wife rests knowing she is protected by a man who loves her fiercely.

Father, I release every selfish ambition and choose to serve my wife as Christ served the church. My hands, my time, my strength are hers.
